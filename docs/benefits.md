# Benefits vs Traditional Markets

Rayo offers significant advantages over both traditional finance (stocks, forex) and centralized crypto exchanges (CEXs). Here's how we compare.

## Rayo vs Traditional Brokerages (CEXs)

### 🔐 Self-Custody - Your Keys, Your Crypto

**Rayo (DEX):**
- ✅ You control your funds at all times
- ✅ No counterparty risk from exchange insolvency
- ✅ Non-custodial trading via Hyperliquid
- ✅ Withdraw anytime without permission

**CEXs (Binance, Coinbase, Kraken):**
- ❌ Exchange holds your funds
- ❌ Risk of exchange hacks or bankruptcy (FTX, Mt.Gox)
- ❌ Withdrawal limits and delays
- ❌ "Not your keys, not your crypto"

**Real Example:** When FTX collapsed in 2022, users lost $8 billion. Rayo users can't lose funds this way because you always control your wallet.

---

### 🌐 No KYC - Trade Anonymously

**Rayo:**
- ✅ No identity verification required
- ✅ No document uploads (passport, ID, selfies)
- ✅ No personal data stored
- ✅ Privacy-preserving

**CEXs:**
- ❌ Mandatory KYC in most jurisdictions
- ❌ Upload government ID, proof of address
- ❌ Facial recognition / selfie verification
- ❌ Your data stored on centralized servers (hack risk)

**Why it matters:**
- Start trading in 30 seconds, not 3 days
- No data breach risk
- Accessible to users in restricted regions

### ⚡ Speed - Trade in Milliseconds

**Rayo (Hyperliquid L1):**
- ✅ Sub-second finality
- ✅ Orders execute in ~200ms
- ✅ On-chain settlement (fully transparent)
- ✅ No withdrawal delays

- ❌ Withdrawals can take hours or days
- ❌ Withdrawal approval processes
- ❌ Off-chain order matching (opaque)

**Bottom line:** Rayo is as fast as CEXs for trading, but WAY faster for withdrawals.

## Trade-offs: Where CEXs Win

To be fair, centralized exchanges do have some advantages:

### CEX Advantages

1. **Fiat On/Off Ramps**
   - CEXs let you deposit USD via bank transfer, credit card
   - Rayo requires you already have crypto (USDC, BTC, ETH)

2. **More Trading Pairs**
   - Binance: 1,000+ trading pairs
   - Rayo (Hyperliquid): 100+ markets (but growing fast)

3. **Customer Support**
   - CEXs have support teams (though quality varies)
   - Rayo is decentralized (community support via Discord/Telegram)

4. **Familiarity**
   - CEXs look like traditional trading platforms
   - DEXs require understanding wallets and blockchain

5. **Insurance Funds**
   - Some CEXs have insurance for hacks (though not for insolvency)
   - Rayo: Smart contract risk exists (though Hyperliquid is battle-tested)
