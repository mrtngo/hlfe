# Deposits & Withdrawals

Learn how to move funds in and out of Rayo safely and efficiently.

## Overview

Rayo supports multiple deposit and withdrawal methods to give you flexibility:

1. **Hyperliquid Bridge** (Arbitrum ↔ Hyperliquid)
2. **Cross-Chain Bridge** (Ethereum, Polygon, Base, Optimism → Arbitrum via Rhino.fi)
3. **Spot Deposits** (BTC, ETH, SOL directly to Hyperliquid)
4. **Withdrawals** (Hyperliquid → Arbitrum → Your wallet)

---

## Deposit Methods

### Method 1: Hyperliquid Bridge (Arbitrum → Hyperliquid)

**Best for:** If you already have USDC on Arbitrum.

#### Step-by-Step

1. **Open Rayo app**
   - Go to Home screen
   - Tap "Deposit"

2. **Select "Bridge" tab**

3. **Enter amount**
   - How much USDC to bridge
   - Minimum: $5

4. **Review details**
   - From: Arbitrum
   - To: Hyperliquid
   - Fee: Gas only (~$0.50)
   - Time: ~30 seconds

5. **Tap "Deposit to Hyperliquid"**

6. **Approve in wallet**
   - Confirm the transaction

7. **Wait for confirmation**
   - Funds appear in ~30 seconds to 2 minutes

8. **✅ Done!**
   - USDC now available to trade

#### Details

**Fee:** Gas fees only (~$0.50 - $2)
**Time:** 30 seconds to 2 minutes
**Minimum:** $5
**Network:** Arbitrum → Hyperliquid

---

### Method 2: Cross-Chain Bridge (Other Chains → Arbitrum)

**Best for:** If you have USDC on Ethereum, Polygon, Base, or Optimism.

**Powered by:** Rhino.fi

#### Step-by-Step

1. **Open Rayo app**
   - Go to Home screen
   - Tap "Deposit"

2. **Select "Cross-Chain Bridge" tab**

3. **Select source chain**
   - Ethereum
   - Polygon
   - Base
   - Optimism

4. **Enter amount**
   - How much USDC to bridge
   - Check minimum (varies by chain)

5. **Review bridge details**
   - Source chain
   - Destination: Arbitrum
   - Fee estimate
   - Time estimate

6. **Tap "Bridge to Arbitrum"**

7. **Approve in wallet**
   - Confirm transaction on source chain

8. **Wait for bridge**
   - 1-3 minutes (sometimes up to 10 minutes)

9. **✅ USDC arrives on Arbitrum**
   - Now use Method 1 to bridge to Hyperliquid

#### Fees & Times

| Source Chain | Fee | Time |
|--------------|-----|------|
| **Ethereum** | $5-$20 (gas dependent) | 1-3 minutes |
| **Polygon** | $0.50-$2 | 1-3 minutes |
| **Base** | $0.50-$2 | 1-3 minutes |
| **Optimism** | $0.50-$2 | 1-3 minutes |

**Note:** Fees shown by Rhino.fi before confirming.

---

### Method 3: Spot Deposits (BTC, ETH, SOL)

**Best for:** If you have BTC, ETH, or SOL and want to deposit them directly.

#### Supported Assets

- **BTC** - Bitcoin
- **ETH** - Ethereum
- **SOL** - Solana

#### Step-by-Step

1. **Open Rayo app**
   - Go to Home screen
   - Tap "Deposit"

2. **Select "Assets" tab**

3. **Choose asset**
   - BTC, ETH, or SOL

4. **View your deposit address**
   - Copy the address
   - OR scan QR code

5. **Send from external wallet**
   - Paste the Hyperliquid deposit address
   - Enter amount to send
   - Double-check address!
   - Send transaction

6. **Wait for confirmations**
   - BTC: 1-3 confirmations (~10-30 mins)
   - ETH: 12-35 confirmations (~3-8 mins)
   - SOL: ~1 minute

7. **✅ Assets appear**
   - View in Profile → Assets

#### Minimums & Fees

| Asset | Minimum Deposit | Network Fee |
|-------|----------------|-------------|
| **BTC** | 0.0001 BTC (~$9) | ~0.00005 BTC (~$4.50) |
| **ETH** | 0.001 ETH (~$3) | ~0.0005 ETH (~$1.50) |
| **SOL** | 0.01 SOL (~$2) | ~0.005 SOL (~$1) |

**Important:** Don't send amounts below minimum - they may be lost!

---

### Method 4: Buy USDC on CEX → Withdraw to Arbitrum

**Best for:** If you have fiat and no crypto yet.

#### Step-by-Step

1. **Buy USDC on a CEX**
   - Coinbase, Binance, Kraken, etc.
   - Deposit fiat (USD, EUR, etc.)
   - Buy USDC

2. **Withdraw USDC**
   - Choose **Arbitrum network**
   - Enter your wallet address (from Privy/MetaMask)
   - Withdraw

3. **USDC arrives in wallet**
   - Check wallet balance

4. **Use Method 1**
   - Bridge from Arbitrum to Hyperliquid

#### Fees

**CEX withdrawal fee:**
- Coinbase: Free (on Arbitrum)
- Binance: $1
- Kraken: $5

**CEX → Arbitrum time:** 10 minutes to 2 hours

## Withdrawal Methods

### Method 1: Hyperliquid → Arbitrum

**Step-by-Step:**

1. **Open Rayo app**
   - Go to Profile
   - Tap "Withdraw"

2. **Enter amount**
   - How much USDC to withdraw
   - Available balance shown

3. **Choose destination**
   - Your Arbitrum wallet (connected wallet)

4. **Review details**
   - Amount
   - Destination address
   - Fee: Gas only (~$0.50)

5. **Tap "Withdraw"**

6. **Confirm transaction**

7. **Wait for withdrawal**
   - ~30 seconds to 2 minutes

8. **✅ USDC in your Arbitrum wallet**
   - Can now send to CEX or use in DeFi

#### Details

**Fee:** Gas fees only (~$0.50 - $2)
**Time:** 30 seconds to 2 minutes
**Destination:** Arbitrum wallet
**Minimum:** $1

---

### Method 2: Withdraw Spot Assets (BTC, ETH, SOL)

**Step-by-Step:**

1. **Go to Profile → Assets**

2. **Select asset** (BTC, ETH, SOL)

3. **Tap "Withdraw"**

4. **Enter destination address**
   - Your external wallet address
   - Triple-check the address!

5. **Enter amount**
   - How much to withdraw

6. **Review**
   - Destination address
   - Amount
   - Network fee

7. **Confirm withdrawal**

8. **Wait for blockchain confirmations**

9. **✅ Assets arrive in external wallet**

#### Fees

**Network fees:**
- BTC: ~0.00005 BTC (~$4.50)
- ETH: ~0.0005 ETH (~$1.50)
- SOL: ~0.005 SOL (~$1)

**Time:**
- BTC: 10-30 minutes
- ETH: 3-8 minutes
- SOL: ~1 minute

---

### Method 3: Arbitrum → CEX (Cash Out)

**If you want to convert to fiat:**

1. **Withdraw from Hyperliquid to Arbitrum** (Method 1 above)

2. **Send USDC from Arbitrum to CEX**
   - Go to your CEX (Coinbase, Binance, etc.)
   - Get your USDC deposit address (Arbitrum network)
   - Send from your Arbitrum wallet

3. **Sell USDC for fiat**
   - Trade USDC → USD (or your currency)

4. **Withdraw fiat to bank**
   - Bank transfer, PayPal, etc.

## Safety & Best Practices

### Before Depositing

**1. Start Small**
- Test with minimum amount first ($5-$10)
- Verify it works before depositing large amounts

**2. Double-Check Addresses**
- Always verify destination address
- One wrong character = funds lost forever

**3. Check Network**
- Ensure you're using correct network (Arbitrum, Hyperliquid, etc.)
- Sending to wrong network = funds lost

**4. Save Transaction Hash**
- Copy TX hash for tracking
- Useful if support needed

---

### Before Withdrawing

**1. Close All Positions**
- Can't withdraw margin locked in positions
- Close positions first

**2. Account for Fees**
- Don't try to withdraw exact balance
- Leave buffer for gas fees

**3. Verify Address**
- Triple-check withdrawal address
- Send test amount first if large withdrawal

**4. Check Network Congestion**
- High congestion = higher fees, longer times
- Use gas trackers (Arbiscan for Arbitrum)

---

### Security Tips

**1. Use Hardware Wallet (Advanced)**
- Store large amounts in Ledger/Trezor
- Connect via WalletConnect for trading

**2. Enable 2FA on CEXs**
- If withdrawing to CEX
- Prevents unauthorized withdrawals

**3. Whitelist Addresses (If Available)**
- Some CEXs allow withdrawal whitelists
- Extra security layer

**4. Beware of Phishing**
- Always use official Rayo app (app.rayo.trade)
- Never share seed phrase or private key
- Double-check URLs

## Troubleshooting

### Deposit Not Showing Up

**If Hyperliquid bridge:**
1. Check TX hash on Arbiscan
2. Verify it was sent to correct address
3. Wait 5 minutes (sometimes delayed)
4. Contact support if >10 minutes

**If cross-chain bridge:**
1. Check Rhino.fi transaction status
2. Bridges can take up to 10 minutes
3. Check destination wallet on Arbiscan

**If spot deposit:**
1. Check blockchain explorer (BTC/ETH/SOL)
2. Verify confirmations (need 1-35 depending on asset)
3. Check you sent to correct address and network

---

### Withdrawal Failed

**Common reasons:**

**1. Insufficient balance**
- You tried to withdraw more than available
- Account for fees

**2. Active positions**
- Can't withdraw margin locked in positions
- Close positions first

**3. Pending orders**
- Cancel open orders that lock funds

**4. Network issues**
- Try again in a few minutes
- Check if network is congested

---

### Wrong Network / Address

**If you sent to wrong address:**
- **Funds are likely lost** (blockchain is irreversible)
- Contact recipient if known
- Contact support (may not be able to help)

**Prevention:**
- Always double-check address
- Send test amount first
- Verify network

## Common Questions

### Can I deposit fiat (USD) directly?

**No.** Rayo doesn't support fiat deposits. You need to:
1. Buy crypto (USDC) on a CEX
2. Withdraw to your wallet
3. Bridge to Hyperliquid

---

### How long do deposits take?

**Arbitrum → Hyperliquid:** 30 seconds to 2 minutes
**Cross-chain bridges:** 1-10 minutes
**Spot deposits:** 1-30 minutes (varies by blockchain)

---

### Can I deposit other stablecoins (USDT, DAI)?

**Not directly.** You need USDC on Arbitrum. If you have other stablecoins:
1. Swap to USDC on a DEX (Uniswap)
2. Bridge to Arbitrum if needed
3. Then bridge to Hyperliquid

---

### Are there withdrawal limits?

**No daily limits** on Rayo/Hyperliquid.

However, large withdrawals (>$100k) may:
- Require additional blockchain confirmations
- Take slightly longer

---

### Can I cancel a deposit/withdrawal?

**No.** Blockchain transactions are irreversible once confirmed.

**Before confirming:**
- Double-check everything
- Once confirmed, can't cancel

---

### Is my money safe?

**Rayo is non-custodial:**
- ✅ You control your funds via your wallet
- ✅ No KYC, no account freezes
- ✅ Withdraw anytime

**Risks:**
- ⚠️ Smart contract risk (Hyperliquid contracts)
- ⚠️ Bridge risk (rare, but possible)
- ⚠️ User error (sending to wrong address)

**Best practices:**
- Only deposit what you can afford to lose
- Start small, test first
- Use hardware wallet for large amounts

## Step-by-Step: First Deposit

**Complete beginner walkthrough:**

### If You Have Fiat (USD, EUR)

1. **Create account on Coinbase/Binance**
2. **Verify identity (KYC)**
3. **Deposit fiat** → Buy USDC
4. **Withdraw USDC to Arbitrum**
   - Choose "Arbitrum" network
   - Enter your wallet address from Rayo
5. **Wait for USDC** to arrive
6. **Open Rayo → Deposit → Bridge**
7. **Bridge USDC to Hyperliquid**
8. **✅ Start trading!**

---

### If You Have Crypto

1. **Ensure you have USDC**
   - If not, swap on Uniswap/1inch
2. **Check which network** (Ethereum, Polygon, etc.)
3. **If not on Arbitrum:**
   - Use Rhino.fi bridge (in Rayo app)
   - Bridge to Arbitrum
4. **Bridge Arbitrum → Hyperliquid**
   - Rayo → Deposit → Bridge
5. **✅ Start trading!**
