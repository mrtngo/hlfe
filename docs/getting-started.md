# Getting Started with Rayo

Get started trading on Rayo in just a few minutes. No KYC, no lengthy registration - just connect your wallet and start trading.

## Prerequisites

Before you begin, you'll need:

- 📱 A mobile device or desktop browser
- 💰 Some USDC for trading (on Arbitrum network)
- 🌐 Internet connection

**That's it!** No account creation, no identity verification required.

## Step 1: Access Rayo

Visit [app.rayo.trade](https://app.rayo.trade) on your mobile browser or desktop.

For the best experience on mobile, we recommend adding Rayo to your home screen:

**iOS (Safari)**:
1. Tap the Share button
2. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen"
3. Tap "Add"

**Android (Chrome)**:
1. Tap the menu (⋮)
2. Tap "Add to Home screen"
3. Tap "Add"

## Step 2: Connect Your Wallet

Rayo uses [Privy](https://privy.io) for wallet connection, giving you multiple options:

### Option A: Create an Embedded Wallet (Easiest)
Perfect for newcomers - no wallet app needed!

1. Click "Connect Wallet" or "Get Started"
2. Choose "Email" or "Phone"
3. Enter your email/phone number
4. Verify with the code sent to you
5. ✅ Done! Your wallet is created and secured

Your embedded wallet is:
- 🔐 **Secure** - Encrypted and non-custodial
- 📱 **Easy** - No seed phrases to manage
- 💾 **Exportable** - You can export your private key anytime

### Option B: Connect Existing Wallet
Already have a crypto wallet? Connect it directly:

1. Click "Connect Wallet"
2. Choose your wallet (MetaMask, WalletConnect, Coinbase Wallet, etc.)
3. Approve the connection
4. ✅ You're in!

## Step 3: Enable Trading (One-Time Setup)

To trade seamlessly without approving every transaction, you need to:

### 3a. Enable Agent Wallet
1. Go to Settings (tap Profile → Settings icon)
2. Find "Agent Wallet"
3. Tap "Activar" (Activate)
4. Approve the transaction in your wallet
5. ✅ Agent wallet active!

**What is this?** The agent wallet allows you to trade without signing every single transaction. It's a huge UX improvement - you approve once, then trade freely.

### 3b. Approve Builder Fee (Optional)
1. In Settings, find "Builder Fee"
2. Tap "Aprobar" (Approve)
3. Approve the transaction

**What is this?** A small 0.03% fee (3 basis points) that supports Rayo development. This is optional but helps us keep improving the platform.

## Step 4: Deposit Funds

You need USDC on Arbitrum to start trading. You have several options:

### Option A: Already Have USDC on Arbitrum
Perfect! Skip to Step 5.

### Option B: Bridge from Another Chain
Use our integrated Rhino.fi bridge:

1. Go to Home → Tap "Deposit"
2. Select "Cross-Chain Bridge" tab
3. Choose your source chain (Ethereum, Polygon, Base, Optimism)
4. Enter the amount of USDC to bridge
5. Tap "Bridge to Arbitrum"
6. Confirm in your wallet
7. Wait 1-3 minutes for the bridge to complete

### Option C: Deposit Directly to Hyperliquid
Bridge from Arbitrum to Hyperliquid:

1. Go to Home → Tap "Deposit"
2. Select "Bridge" tab
3. Enter amount of USDC
4. Tap "Deposit to Hyperliquid"
5. Confirm the transaction
6. ✅ Funds appear in ~30 seconds

### Option D: Spot Deposit (BTC, ETH, SOL)
Deposit crypto directly to Hyperliquid:

1. Go to Home → Tap "Deposit"
2. Select "Assets" tab
3. Choose BTC, ETH, or SOL
4. Get your deposit address
5. Send from your external wallet
6. ✅ Funds appear after confirmations

**Minimum deposits:**
- BTC: 0.0001 BTC (Fee: ~0.00005 BTC)
- ETH: 0.001 ETH (Fee: ~0.0005 ETH)
- SOL: 0.01 SOL (Fee: ~0.005 SOL)
- USDC: $5 (Network fees apply)

## Step 5: Make Your First Trade

Now you're ready to trade!

### Quick Trade (Basic Mode)
1. Tap "Tradear" (Trade) in the bottom navigation
2. Choose a market (e.g., BTC-USD)
3. Select "Long" (buy) or "Short" (sell)
4. Enter your margin amount in USD
5. Choose your leverage (1x to 50x)
6. Tap the big yellow button to place your order
7. ✅ Position opened!

### Advanced Trading
For limit orders, stop loss/take profit, and more advanced features, see our [Trading Guide](trading-guide.md).

## Step 6: Monitor Your Position

- View open positions on the Trading page
- Set Stop Loss / Take Profit by tapping "SL/TP" button
- Close position anytime by tapping "Cerrar" (Close)
- Track your P&L in real-time

## Step 7: Withdraw Profits

When you're ready to withdraw:

1. Go to Profile
2. Tap "Withdraw"
3. Enter amount
4. Choose destination (back to Arbitrum wallet)
5. Confirm transaction
6. ✅ Funds withdrawn!

## Tips for Beginners

1. **Start small** - Test with small amounts first
2. **Use lower leverage** - 2-5x is safer than 50x for beginners
3. **Set stop losses** - Protect yourself from large losses
4. **Learn first** - Read our [Trading Guide](trading-guide.md) before risking real money
5. **Never trade emotionally** - Have a plan and stick to it
