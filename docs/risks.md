# Risks & Disclaimers

## ⚠️ IMPORTANT: READ BEFORE TRADING

Trading leveraged derivatives is **extremely risky** and can result in the **total loss of your funds**. This page outlines the risks you need to understand before using Rayo.

## Risk Warning

### You Can Lose Everything

- **Leverage amplifies losses** - With 10x leverage, a 10% move against you = 100% loss
- **Liquidation is permanent** - If the market moves against your position, you can be liquidated and lose your entire margin
- **No refunds** - All trades are final and executed on-chain
- **Market volatility** - Crypto markets can move 10-20% in minutes

### High-Risk Trading

Rayo enables you to trade perpetual futures with leverage up to **50x**. This means:

✅ **Potential for high returns** - Small price movements can generate large profits
❌ **Potential for total loss** - Small price movements can wipe out your entire position

**Example of leverage risk:**
- You deposit $100 and use 20x leverage to open a $2,000 position
- If the price moves **5% against you**, you lose 100% ($100 liquidated)
- If the price moves **5% in your favor**, you gain 100% ($100 profit)

## Specific Risks

### 1. Liquidation Risk

**What is liquidation?**
When your position loses enough value that your margin is no longer sufficient to maintain it, you get liquidated. Your position is automatically closed and you lose your margin.

**Liquidation triggers:**
- **Long positions**: Price drops below your liquidation price
- **Short positions**: Price rises above your liquidation price

**Protection:**
- Use lower leverage (2-5x instead of 20-50x)
- Set stop-loss orders
- Monitor positions actively
- Never use 100% of your available margin

### 2. Market Risk

**Extreme volatility:**
- Crypto markets can gap 10-30% overnight
- Flash crashes can trigger mass liquidations
- News events cause rapid price swings
- No circuit breakers like traditional markets

**Illiquidity:**
- Some markets may have low liquidity
- Large orders can experience slippage
- During extreme volatility, spreads widen

### 3. Smart Contract Risk

**Code vulnerabilities:**
- Hyperliquid smart contracts could have bugs
- Rayo smart contract integrations could fail
- Bridges could be exploited

**While unlikely, smart contract risks include:**
- Loss of funds due to exploits
- Contract upgrades changing functionality
- Oracle failures causing incorrect liquidations

### 4. Bridge Risk

**Cross-chain bridge risks:**
- Rhino.fi bridge could fail or be exploited
- Funds could be lost during bridging
- Bridge delays could cause missed opportunities

**Mitigation:**
- Only bridge amounts you're ready to trade
- Wait for bridge confirmations before trading
- Use established bridges with track records

### 5. Custody Risk

**Embedded wallets:**
- Your email/phone wallet is encrypted but depends on Privy's infrastructure
- If you lose access to your email/phone, recovery may be difficult
- Always export and backup your private key

**External wallets:**
- You are responsible for your wallet security
- Lost seed phrases = lost funds forever
- Phishing attacks can steal your wallet

### 6. Platform Risk

**Rayo-specific risks:**
- Platform bugs could affect trading
- Downtime during critical moments
- UI/UX issues leading to unintended trades
- Being a new platform, unexpected issues may arise

**Hyperliquid-specific risks:**
- Exchange downtime (rare but possible)
- API issues affecting order execution
- Network congestion causing delays

### 7. Regulatory Risk

**Legal uncertainty:**
- Crypto regulations vary by jurisdiction
- Trading may become restricted in your country
- Tax implications can be complex
- No regulatory protections like traditional brokerages

**Your responsibility:**
- Ensure trading is legal in your jurisdiction
- Pay applicable taxes on profits
- Understand local regulations

### 8. Funding Rate Risk

**Perpetual futures funding:**
- You pay funding rates to shorts (if long) or receive from longs (if short)
- Funding rates can be 0.01% - 0.1% per 8 hours
- In extreme markets, funding can be 1%+ per day
- Holding positions long-term costs money via funding

### 9. Price Manipulation

**Possible market manipulation:**
- Whale manipulation in thin markets
- Wash trading affecting price discovery
- Oracle manipulation (theoretical)
- Front-running of large orders

### 10. Operational Risk

**User errors:**
- Placing wrong order sizes (fat finger errors)
- Using excessive leverage by mistake
- Forgetting open positions
- Not setting stop-losses

**Prevention:**
- Double-check every order before submitting
- Start with small sizes
- Use limit orders when possible
- Set alerts for position P&L

## Risk Management Best Practices

If you decide to trade despite these risks:

### 1. Capital Management
- **Never risk more than 1-2% per trade**
- Keep 50%+ of account in reserve (don't use 100% margin)
- Have emergency funds outside of crypto

### 2. Position Sizing
- Start with 2-5x leverage maximum
- Use smaller position sizes (0.5-1% of account)
- Increase size only with experience

### 3. Stop Losses
- **ALWAYS set stop-losses**
- Place stops at 2-5% loss maximum
- Move stops to breakeven after profit

### 4. Leverage Rules
- Beginners: 2-5x maximum
- Intermediate: 5-10x maximum
- Advanced: 10-20x maximum
- **Avoid 30-50x unless you're an expert**

### 5. Diversification
- Don't put all funds in one position
- Trade multiple uncorrelated markets
- Keep funds on multiple platforms

### 6. Emotional Control
- Never revenge trade after losses
- Take breaks after 2-3 losing trades
- Don't overtrade (max 3-5 trades per day)
- Stick to your trading plan

### 7. Continuous Learning
- Keep a trading journal
- Review losing trades
- Learn from mistakes
- Study market behavior
