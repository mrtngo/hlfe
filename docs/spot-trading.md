# Spot Trading

Learn how to buy and sell cryptocurrencies directly on Rayo's spot exchange powered by Hyperliquid.

## What is Spot Trading?

Spot trading is buying and selling cryptocurrency for **immediate delivery**. Unlike futures, you **own the actual asset**.

### Spot vs Futures

| Feature | Spot Trading | Futures Trading |
|---------|-------------|-----------------|
| **Ownership** | ✅ You own the asset | ❌ You don't own it |
| **Leverage** | 1x (no leverage) | Up to 50x |
| **Expiration** | Never | Never (perpetual) |
| **Funding Rates** | No funding | Yes, every 8h |
| **Short Selling** | Must borrow | Native shorts |
| **Risk** | Lower (can't be liquidated) | Higher (liquidation risk) |
| **Use Case** | Long-term holding, DCA | Trading, speculation |

---

## Why Trade Spot?

### Benefits

**1. No Liquidation Risk**
- Can't lose more than you invest
- No margin calls
- Hold through volatility

**2. No Funding Rates**
- No ongoing costs for holding
- Perfect for long-term holds

**3. Actual Ownership**
- Withdraw tokens to external wallet
- Use in DeFi protocols
- Stake for rewards (on some assets)

**4. Simpler**
- No leverage calculations
- No liquidation prices
- Easier for beginners

---

### When to Use Spot Instead of Futures

**Use Spot if:**
- ✅ You're bullish long-term (months/years)
- ✅ You want to avoid funding costs
- ✅ You're doing dollar-cost averaging (DCA)
- ✅ You plan to withdraw and use tokens elsewhere
- ✅ You want minimal risk

**Use Futures if:**
- ✅ You want to use leverage
- ✅ You want to go short
- ✅ You're trading short-term (hours/days)
- ✅ You want capital efficiency

## How to Trade Spot

### Accessing Spot Trading

1. Open Rayo app
2. Tap **"Spot"** in bottom navigation
3. Select the asset you want to trade

---

### Buying Crypto (Spot)

**Step-by-step:**

1. **Select asset**
   - Choose the crypto you want to buy (e.g., BTC, ETH, SOL)

2. **Choose order type**
   - **Market:** Buy immediately at current price
   - **Limit:** Buy only at your specified price

3. **Enter amount**
   - Enter how much USDC you want to spend
   - OR enter how much of the asset you want to buy

4. **Review order**
   - Check price
   - Check fees (0.045% market order)
   - Check total cost

5. **Place order**
   - Tap "Comprar" (Buy)
   - Confirm transaction

6. **Assets received**
   - Tokens appear in your wallet immediately
   - View in Profile → Assets

---

### Selling Crypto (Spot)

**Step-by-step:**

1. **Select asset**
   - Choose the crypto you want to sell

2. **Choose order type**
   - **Market:** Sell immediately
   - **Limit:** Sell only at your specified price

3. **Enter amount**
   - Enter how much of the asset to sell
   - Example: Sell 0.5 ETH

4. **Review order**
   - Check price
   - Check fees
   - Check USDC you'll receive

5. **Place order**
   - Tap "Vender" (Sell)
   - Confirm transaction

6. **USDC received**
   - USDC added to your balance
   - Available to trade or withdraw

## Order Types

### Market Order

**What it does:** Executes immediately at the best available price.

**Use when:**
- You want to buy/sell right now
- Price is acceptable
- Order size is small (less slippage)

**Example:**
- BTC price: $96,000
- You place market buy for $1,000
- You receive: ~0.0104 BTC (minus 0.045% fee)

**Pros:**
- ✅ Instant execution
- ✅ Guaranteed to fill

**Cons:**
- ❌ Possible slippage on large orders
- ❌ Higher fee (0.045%)

---

### Limit Order

**What it does:** Only executes at your specified price or better.

**Use when:**
- You want a specific entry/exit price
- You're not in a rush
- You want to save on fees

**Example:**
- BTC price: $96,000
- You place limit buy at $95,000
- If BTC drops to $95,000 → Order fills
- If BTC stays above $95,000 → Order stays pending

**Pros:**
- ✅ Price control
- ✅ Lower fee (0.02% maker fee if you add liquidity)
- ✅ No slippage

**Cons:**
- ❌ May not fill
- ❌ Requires patience

## Strategies

### 1. Dollar-Cost Averaging (DCA)

**What it is:** Buy a fixed amount regularly, regardless of price.

**Example:**
- Buy $100 of BTC every Monday
- Over 12 weeks = $1,200 total
- Average cost smooths out volatility

**Benefits:**
- ✅ Removes emotional decisions
- ✅ Lowers average cost in volatile markets
- ✅ Disciplined approach

**How to do it on Rayo:**
- Set a reminder (Monday 9am)
- Buy $100 BTC every week
- Don't check price, just execute

---

### 2. Buy the Dip

**What it is:** Buy when price drops significantly.

**Example:**
- BTC normally trades $95,000 - $100,000
- BTC crashes to $85,000 (-15%)
- You buy the dip

**Rules:**
- Only buy dips in assets you believe in long-term
- Don't catch falling knives (wait for reversal signs)
- Use limit orders at support levels

**Risk management:**
- Don't use all capital on first dip
- Scale in: 25% at -10%, 25% at -20%, etc.

---

### 3. Swing Trading (Spot)

**What it is:** Buy low, sell high on multi-day swings.

**Example:**
- ETH at support: $3,000 (buy)
- ETH at resistance: $3,300 (sell)
- Profit: 10% - fees

**Requirements:**
- Technical analysis skills
- Patience (hold for days/weeks)
- Risk management (stop losses)

**Advantages over futures:**
- No funding costs
- No liquidation risk
- Can hold through volatility

---

### 4. HODLing

**What it is:** Buy and hold for months/years.

**Example:**
- Buy $10,000 of BTC
- Hold for 4+ years through cycles
- Don't sell during bear markets

**Psychology:**
- Ignore short-term volatility
- Believe in long-term adoption
- Accumulate during bear markets

**Why spot is perfect:**
- No funding costs eating profits
- No liquidation risk during crashes
- Can stake/lend for passive income (if available)

---

### 5. Portfolio Rebalancing

**What it is:** Maintain target allocations by buying/selling.

**Example:**
- Target: 50% BTC, 30% ETH, 20% SOL
- BTC pumps → Now 60% BTC
- Sell 10% BTC, buy more ETH/SOL
- Rebalance back to 50/30/20

**Benefits:**
- ✅ Takes profits on winners
- ✅ Buys dips on losers
- ✅ Maintains risk profile

**Frequency:**
- Rebalance monthly or quarterly
- Or when allocations drift >10%

## Risk Management

### Diversification

**Don't put all funds in one asset.**

**Example portfolio:**
- 40% BTC (large cap, low risk)
- 30% ETH (large cap, medium risk)
- 20% SOL/AVAX/MATIC (mid cap, higher risk)
- 10% Small caps (high risk, high reward)

**Why:**
- If one asset crashes, others may hold value
- Different assets perform well at different times

---

### Position Sizing

**Rule:** Don't allocate more than 10-20% to any single asset (except BTC/ETH).

**Example:**
- Portfolio: $10,000
- Max per asset: $1,000 (10%)

**Exceptions:**
- BTC: Can be 30-50% of portfolio (most stable)
- ETH: Can be 20-30% (second most stable)
- Others: 5-15% each

---

### Stop Losses (Mental or Hard)

Even in spot trading, use stop losses to limit downside.

**Mental stop loss:**
- "If BTC drops below $90,000, I sell"
- Requires discipline

**Hard stop loss:**
- Place a stop market order
- Auto-sells if price hits stop

**Example:**
- Buy BTC at $96,000
- Set mental stop at $86,400 (-10%)
- If it hits, sell and preserve capital

---

### Taking Profits

**Don't be greedy.**

**Strategies:**

**1. Fixed targets:**
- Sell 50% at +50%
- Sell 25% at +100%
- Let 25% ride

**2. Trailing stops:**
- After +50%, set stop at +30%
- Locks in gains while letting winners run

**3. Rebalancing:**
- When BTC allocation grows too large, trim and diversify

## Advanced Spot Trading

### Limit Order Strategies

#### 1. Laddering Buys

**Strategy:** Place multiple limit orders at different prices.

**Example:**
- Current BTC price: $96,000
- Place buy orders:
  - $95,000: $500
  - $94,000: $500
  - $93,000: $500
  - $92,000: $500

**Benefit:** Average down if price drops, but don't use all capital at once.

---

#### 2. Laddering Sells

**Strategy:** Take profits at multiple levels.

**Example:**
- You bought BTC at $90,000
- Place sell orders:
  - $100,000: 25% (11% profit)
  - $110,000: 25% (22% profit)
  - $120,000: 25% (33% profit)
  - Let 25% run

**Benefit:** Lock in profits while keeping upside exposure.

---

### Understanding Orderbook Depth

**What it shows:** Buy and sell orders at different price levels.

**How to read:**
- **Bids (buy orders):** Green, below current price
- **Asks (sell orders):** Red, above current price

**Large walls:**
- **Buy wall** at $95,000 → Strong support
- **Sell wall** at $97,000 → Strong resistance

**Trading tip:**
- Place limit buys just above buy walls (likely support)
- Place limit sells just below sell walls (likely resistance)

---

### Slippage Management

**What is slippage?**
Price movement between when you place an order and when it fills.

**Example:**
- You want to buy $100,000 of BTC
- Current price: $96,000
- Low liquidity → Price moves to $96,200 while filling
- You paid $200 more than expected

**How to minimize:**
- Use limit orders (no slippage, but may not fill)
- Trade during high liquidity periods
- Split large orders into smaller chunks

## Spot Trading vs Spot Holdings

### Trading Spot

**Goal:** Profit from price swings.

**Characteristics:**
- Frequent buying/selling
- Focus on technical analysis
- Shorter holding periods (days/weeks)

**Tax implications:**
- Short-term capital gains (higher tax)

---

### Holding Spot (Investing)

**Goal:** Long-term wealth building.

**Characteristics:**
- Infrequent trading
- Focus on fundamentals
- Longer holding periods (months/years)

**Tax implications:**
- Long-term capital gains (lower tax, if held >1 year in most jurisdictions)

## Common Mistakes

### 1. FOMOing Into Pumps

**Mistake:** BTC pumps 20%, you buy at the top.

**Reality:** Price corrects 10%, you're down.

**Fix:** Wait for pullbacks, use limit orders, be patient.

---

### 2. Panic Selling Dips

**Mistake:** BTC drops 15%, you panic sell.

**Reality:** Price recovers next week, you missed the rebound.

**Fix:** Have a plan before buying. If fundamentals haven't changed, hold.

---

### 3. Not Taking Profits

**Mistake:** Asset is up 200%, you hold expecting more.

**Reality:** Price crashes, your gains evaporate.

**Fix:** Take partial profits on the way up (ladder sells).

---

### 4. Over-Diversifying

**Mistake:** Owning 30 different cryptocurrencies.

**Reality:** Too hard to track, many will go to zero.

**Fix:** Focus on 5-10 quality projects, allocate meaningfully.

---

### 5. Not Tracking Cost Basis

**Mistake:** Buying/selling without tracking entry prices.

**Reality:** Tax time is a nightmare, no idea if you're profitable.

**Fix:** Use Rayo's trade history, export to spreadsheet, track cost basis.

---

## Tax Considerations

### Taxable Events

In most jurisdictions, these trigger taxes:

**1. Selling crypto for fiat (USDC)**
- Buy BTC at $90k, sell at $100k → $10k taxable gain

**2. Trading crypto for crypto**
- Trade BTC for ETH → Taxable event (BTC → USDC → ETH)

**3. Using crypto for purchases**
- Buy something with BTC → Taxable event

**Not taxable:**
- Buying crypto with fiat
- Transferring between your own wallets
- HODLing (until you sell)

---

### Record Keeping

**What to track:**
- Date of purchase
- Amount purchased
- Price at purchase (cost basis)
- Date of sale
- Amount sold
- Price at sale
- Profit/loss

**How:**
- Export trade history from Rayo
- Use crypto tax software (Koinly, CoinTracker, etc.)
- Keep records for at least 7 years
